## Design Context

### Users

STAY serves older residents such as Sarah and the trusted relatives, neighbors, coordinators, and aides in their Circle. The interface must be understandable at a glance and fully usable by touch, keyboard, screen reader, or voice.

### Brand Personality

Calm, dignified, warm, dependable, and resident-controlled. STAY must never feel clinical, patronizing, surveillance-heavy, or alarmist.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with the Chalk, Coal, Pine, Sea Glass, Clay, Mustard, and Emergency palette. Prefer warm light and dark surfaces, clear structure, and restrained 150–220 ms state motion. Avoid sparkles, gradients, glass effects, chatbot decoration, generic AI motifs, excessive pills, and dashboard card soup.

### Design Principles

- Put resident agency and safety clarity before decoration.
- Preserve the same critical functionality on mobile, desktop, touch, keyboard, screen reader, and voice surfaces.
- Use progressive disclosure for detail while keeping current state and next actions visible.
- Maintain at least 48 by 48 pixel touch targets and strong focus, contrast, and text enlargement behavior.
- Make provenance explicit wherever a provider is simulated or unavailable.
- Use logical CSS properties for future RTL support and honor reduced-motion preferences.
