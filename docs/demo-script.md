# Three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — The ordinary day

Open Today on a desktop or tablet. Say: “STAY is for the ordinary hours that make independence possible. Sarah sees one thing, not a wall of monitoring.” Show One Thing, Home Check, calendar, and simulated provider labels.

## 0:25–0:50 — Adaptive access and privacy

Open Access. Toggle captions, One Thing Mode, and reduced motion. Open Privacy and show incident-only location and the permission matrix. Say: “Preferences change presentation, never safety policy.”

## 0:50–1:55 — Protected moment

Return to the protected demo card.

1. Click **Miss the first check**. Show the first deterministic retry and ten-minute grace.
2. Click **Miss the second check**. The Circle incident opens. Say: “Bedrock is not making this decision; Sarah’s saved plan is.”
3. Click **Sarah asks Tom**. Show the minimal update and immutable timeline.
4. Click **Tom accepts**. Pause on **“Tom is on the way.”** Show that Tom owns the response and the travel estimate is labeled simulated.

Say: “No emergency service was contacted. STAY coordinates the people Sarah chose.”

## 1:55–2:30 — Breadth

Show Help Board, Circle roles/availability, House Memory sensitivity, and one Power Outage playbook step. Emphasize that weather, devices, utilities, and maintenance are explicit adapters, not fake live integrations.

## 2:30–2:55 — Alexa+ and AWS

Show the Alexa conversation/caption simulator and widget. Briefly show the architecture: OAuth-aware MCP, Cognito, DynamoDB transaction/outbox, Scheduler, EventBridge, WebSockets, minimal SES, CloudWatch, and the Strands/Nova feature gate.

## 2:55–3:00 — Close

“STAY helps people stay at home, stay connected, and stay in control.”
