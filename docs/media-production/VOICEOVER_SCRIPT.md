# English voice-over script

Target delivery: warm, grounded, precise, 135–145 words per minute. Avoid a promotional announcer voice. Estimated length: 02:32.

## Script

Independent living is often lost in the space between “everything is fine” and an emergency: one missed check-in, one small household problem, or one favor nobody clearly owns.

STAY gives Sarah one calm place for that middle ground. Today shows one thing at a time, an ordinary home check, and useful context. Every weather, device, utility, maintenance, and travel signal in this demo is clearly labeled as simulated.

Access preferences make the same experience work voice-first or touch-first. Sarah can choose captions, more response time, higher contrast, reduced motion, and repeated key information. These settings change presentation. They never change her safety policy.

Privacy is scoped by role and moment. Routine details stay private. Access instructions and location can appear only inside an authenticated, assigned incident when Sarah’s plan permits it.

Now Sarah misses her Morning Safety Window. STAY makes one deterministic check, waits through the grace period, and checks once more. Bedrock does not choose what happens next. Sarah’s saved plan does.

After the second missed check, her Circle plan opens an incident. STAY has not contacted emergency services. Sarah asks Tom, a nearby helper. Only the minimum incident detail is shared, and every action enters the timeline.

Tom accepts. Tom is on the way. He now owns the response, and the Circle sees the same update.

STAY also handles ordinary Help Requests, household playbooks, availability, and privacy-filtered House Memory. The deterministic workflows keep working if the language model is unavailable.

For Alexa Plus, STAY exposes ten goal-level tools through a standards-based MCP server with accessible text, typed structured content, provenance, and Alexa-style widgets. On AWS, Cognito protects identity; DynamoDB transactions and an outbox preserve state; Scheduler and EventBridge coordinate checks; WebSockets and minimal email deliver updates; and Nova interprets only redacted, non-critical intent.

STAY does not contact emergency services, diagnose a condition, detect a fall, or replace Alexa Emergency Assist.

STAY helps people stay at home, stay connected, and stay in control.
