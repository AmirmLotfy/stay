# STAY demo-film production brief

## Deliverable

- English product demonstration for the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon.
- Final runtime: 02:20–02:50; never exceed 02:59.
- Master: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 video with AAC audio in MP4.
- Public destination: user-owned YouTube upload.
- Purpose: prove a working product, not simulate a lifestyle commercial.

## Story

STAY protects the ordinary middle ground between “everything is fine” and an emergency. Sarah misses a resident-defined Safety Window. Two deterministic checks occur before her saved Circle plan starts. Sarah asks Tom, Tom accepts, and the product lands on the protected line: “Tom is on the way.”

## Creative direction

- Calm, warm, legible, and human; the interface remains the hero.
- Use direct screen evidence for product claims.
- Avoid generic AI imagery, fake device footage, medical drama, fear cues, or rescue-service imagery.
- Use restrained motion, simple chapter cards, and only enough music to support pacing.
- Every simulated provider label must remain visible where relevant.

## Safety language

STAY coordinates only the resident’s preconfigured Circle. It does not contact emergency services, diagnose a condition, detect a fall, or replace Alexa Emergency Assist.

## Locked closing line

“STAY helps people stay at home, stay connected, and stay in control.”
